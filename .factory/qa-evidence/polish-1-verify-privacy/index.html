Plain-language policy · Effective 28 August 2026

How your profile data stays local.

Reading Comfort Profiles is designed so your reading preferences do not need to leave your browser.

What the extension stores

The extension stores profile names, text values, contrast choices, focus choices, pointer choices, table spacing, and work-site assignments. It saves this data in your browser’s local extension storage.

What we do not collect

We do not collect browsing history, page content, search queries, profile data, identifiers, or usage analytics. There is no account, advertising, telemetry, tracking pixel, or third-party script.

Permissions

The extension needs website access to apply profiles to text, code, focus indicators, and tables. Active-tab access identifies the work site shown in the popup. Storage access saves your choices locally. The extension does not edit or transmit page text.

Deleting your data

Delete individual custom profiles in the popup. To remove the extension, use Chromium’s extension manager.

This website

The product website has no analytics, cookies, accounts, forms, or third-party resources. Its service worker caches page files for offline viewing. You can clear them through Chromium’s site-data settings.

Questions

Open an issue in the public source repository ↗ for privacy questions or to report a concern.
