Plain-language policy · Effective 28 August 2026

Privacy, fitted locally.

Reading Comfort Profiles is designed so your reading preferences do not need to leave your browser.

What the extension stores

The extension stores profile names, typography values, contrast choices, focus and cursor preferences, table-spacing choices, and the domain-to-profile assignments you create. This data is saved with the browser’s local extension storage API.

What we do not collect

We do not collect browsing history, page content, search queries, profile data, identifiers, or usage analytics. There is no account, advertising, telemetry, tracking pixel, or third-party runtime script.

Permissions

The extension needs access to websites so it can apply the profile you choose to semantic text, code, focus indicators, and tables. Active-tab and tabs access identifies the domain shown in the popup. Storage access saves your choices locally. The extension does not scrape or transmit page content.

Deleting your data

Delete individual custom profiles in the popup. Removing the extension through your browser’s extension manager deletes its local data.

This website

The product website has no analytics, cookies, accounts, forms, or third-party resources. Its service worker caches public page files on your device for reliable offline viewing; clearing site data removes that cache.

Questions

Open an issue in the public source repository for privacy questions or to report a concern.
